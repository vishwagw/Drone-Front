Drone Front

Fly an armed quadcopter from a forward operating base. Each sortie is a new battlefield with its own objective, from a qualification run on the test range to breaking an armoured push and bringing down a drone carrier at night. Finish a sortie to unlock the next.

Online world · always on

Frontier

A shared world that never stops. Fly in, fight whoever is there and leave when you like. The server checks every hit, and your kills, credits and XP stay on your pilot.

Online arena · free-for-all

Fight other pilots

Everyone who joins the same arena code flies in one match. First to 10 kills wins the round, then a new round starts. The pad in the middle of the map repairs your hull and reloads rockets.
